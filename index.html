Municipal & public sector

Built to work the way government works.

Cities, counties, utility authorities, water control districts, fire districts, libraries, and first-responder agencies have requirements most IT shops have never dealt with. We have, and we structure our work around them.

Public dollars come with public accountability. That means clear scopes of work, fixed pricing that fits a budget cycle, documentation your clerk and auditor can use, and work that stands up to a public-records request.

Jan 1
2025deadline passed

Under Florida Statute 282.3185, every municipality under 25,000 residents was required to adopt NIST-consistent cybersecurity standards by this date, train every employee with network access within 30 days of hire and annually, and be ready to report incidents to the state. If your standards aren’t written down and your training isn’t logged, we can get you there quickly.

Procurement-ready. W-9, certificate of insurance, and references are ready on request. We respond to RFQs, RFPs, and informal quotes, monitor Bidnet Direct for Okeechobee County solicitations, and can work under cooperative or piggyback purchasing agreements where your purchasing policy allows.

About

Local first. That’s the whole point.

Big Water Technology is the managed-services practice of Competence Collective LLC, a Florida company, and it exists for one reason: the organizations between Lake Okeechobee and the Indian River Lagoon have been served by providers an hour or more away for too long. When something breaks, a truck from Orlando or West Palm is not the same as a technician who was already in town.

We live here, bank here, and shop here. Our team knows the roads, the growing seasons, the hurricane routine, and the people who run things.
